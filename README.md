# Revenue Intelligence Agent

Founders usually don’t have a shortage of dashboards.
They have a shortage of clarity.

Sales data is spread across CRMs, emails, spreadsheets, meetings, and follow-ups. By the time problems become visible, important deals are already stalled and revenue targets are at risk.

I built this project to explore a simple idea:

> Can we create a system that tells founders what actually needs attention instead of just showing more numbers?

This project takes pipeline data, identifies risks in the sales funnel, estimates expected revenue, and generates a clean founder-style briefing report in HTML format.

---

## What It Does

The agent:

* reads deal and pipeline data
* detects inactive or risky deals
* estimates expected revenue
* flags possible pipeline issues
* generates actionable recommendations
* creates a visually clean executive report that can be sent directly over email

---

## Example Insights

Some examples of insights generated by the system:

* 3 high-value deals have had no activity for more than 10 days
* Expected revenue is tracking below target
* Proposal-stage deals are taking longer than usual to close
* A large portion of the pipeline is inactive

Instead of dumping raw metrics, the report tries to answer:

> “What should the founder focus on right now?”

## Why I Built This

While working in B2B and construction-tech environments, I noticed that leadership teams often spend too much time collecting information and very little time acting on it.

The idea behind this project was to move one step closer to:

* operational visibility
* decision support
* proactive intervention

rather than another static dashboard.
